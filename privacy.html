Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 20, 2026

1. Commitment to Privacy

At PyBloSoft, we believe that privacy is a fundamental right, particularly regarding security tools. Shanbit is built on the principle of "Privacy by Design," meaning the application is architecturally incapable of collecting or storing your personal data.

2. Zero Data Collection

Shanbit does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • • Generated passwords, passphrases, or encryption keys.
  • • Usernames, email addresses, or IP addresses.
  • • Usage statistics, telemetry, or diagnostic data.
  • • Device identifiers or hardware information.

3. 100% Offline Architecture

The application operates entirely without an internet connection. All cryptographic computations are performed locally on your machine. There is no cloud component, ensuring that your sensitive information never leaves your device.

4. Volatile Memory Management

To prevent forensic data recovery, Shanbit manages all session data in Volatile RAM. Your session history is permanently wiped upon closing the application. Additionally, the application includes an automatic clipboard clearing mechanism to protect your data from local system leaks.

5. Third-Party Services

Since Shanbit does not collect data, no information is ever shared with third parties. We do not use advertising identifiers, trackers, or any third-party analytics SDKs within the application.
